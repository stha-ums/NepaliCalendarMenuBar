Stay connected to the Nepali calendar right from your macOS menu bar.

BS Calendar is a beautiful, lightweight utility designed to make viewing Bikram Sambat (BS) dates seamless on your Mac. Whether you need to check today's Nepali date at a glance or plan around important dates, this app has you covered.

Key Features:
*   Menu Bar Access: See the current Nepali date, day, and month instantly in your menu bar.
*   Interactive Calendar: Click to view a full monthly calendar with upcoming events.
*   Calendar Integration: View your macOS Calendar events displayed with Nepali dates.
*   Multiple Views: Switch between monthly grid and timeline schedule modes.
*   Multi-Language Support: Choose between English and Nepali (नेपाली) with proper numerals.
*   Customizable Display: Select from short, long, or full date format options.
*   Offline & Private: Works completely offline with no data collection. Your privacy is guaranteed.
*   Open Source: Fully open-source and transparent.

Perfect for Nepalis living abroad or anyone who needs to keep track of the Bikram Sambat calendar for festivals, official work, or daily life.
